# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Participants must:

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent and discuss ideas without personal attacks.
- Welcome contributors with different experience levels and perspectives.
- Keep project discussions relevant and professional.
- Protect private data and avoid publishing credentials or sensitive records.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disruption, and other abusive
behavior are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions that violate
this policy. Serious or repeated violations may result in a permanent ban from
the project.

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the repository maintainers.
